#### 3. Life-oriented problems:
- (i) If the cost of 1 sq.m of a plot is ₹ 800, then find the total cost of the plot that is 15 m long and 10 m breadth.
- (ii) The side of a square is 6 cm. The length of a rectangle is 10 cm and its breadth is 4 cm. Find the perimeter and area of both the square and rectangle.
- (iii) What will be the labour cost of laying the floor of an assembly hall which is 14 m long and 10 m breadth if the cost of laying is ₹ 60 per sq.m?
