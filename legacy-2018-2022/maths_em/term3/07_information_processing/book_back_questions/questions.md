#### 2. Problem Solving:

#### 3. Activity Analysis:
- Ezhilan and Iniyan have been asked to arrange two sets of fifty books each in two bookshelves of a library. The books are numbered from 1 to 50 and there are 5 shelves in each cupboard. Ezhilan arranged 10 books in each shelf at once while Iniyan placed 1 book at a time in each of the 5 shelves. Which is the easier way to complete the work and who will arrange the books quickly?
