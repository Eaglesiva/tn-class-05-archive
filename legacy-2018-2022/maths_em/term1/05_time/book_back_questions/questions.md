- Morning school break time to morning school ending time
- Morning school working time
- Afternoon school working time
- Afternoon lunch break time

#### 2. Match the following (12-hour clock to 24-hour clock):
1. 9:40 a.m. - a) 23:40 Hours
2. 3:20 p.m. - b) 6:25 Hours
3. 6:25 p.m. - c) 15:20 Hours

#### 3. Addition:
- a) 4 hours 30 minutes + 2 hours 50 minutes = ____________
- b) 4 hours 50 minutes + 2 hours 30 minutes = ____________
- c) 3 hours 45 minutes + 1 hour 35 minutes = ____________
- d) 1 hour 50 minutes + 3 hours 45 minutes = ____________
- e) 2 hours 25 minutes + 4 hours 50 minutes = ____________

- a) 5 hours 10 minutes - 2 hours 35 minutes = ____________
- b) 4 hours 20 minutes - 2 hours 40 minutes = ____________
- c) 4 hours 25 minutes - 1 hour 20 minutes = ____________

#### 5. Answer the following word problems:
- a) An office functions from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. How many hours does the office function?
- b) A school runs from 9:00 a.m. to 4:10 p.m. How many hours does the school work?
- c) A circus show starts at 12:15 p.m. and ends after 2 hours 30 minutes. At what time does the circus show end?
- d) A bank operates from 9:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. What is the total operating time of the bank?
- e) A person travels from Ahmedabad to his hometown in Tamil Nadu. He traveled 2 hours 15 minutes by flight and 4 hours 40 minutes by car. What is the total time of his journey?
- f) A painter painted a house for 3 hours 15 minutes in the morning and 2 hours 50 minutes in the evening. What is the total time he spent on painting?
