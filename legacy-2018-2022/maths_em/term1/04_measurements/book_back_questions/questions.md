#### 7. Answer the following word problems:
- a) Saravanan decided to travel from Puducherry to Chennai in his car. The distance is 165 km. When he started, the odometer read 000157 km. What did the odometer read when he reached Chennai?
- b) Karthik Raja decided to start from A. If he moves towards east, he reaches B; then if he moves 2 km towards north, he reaches C; then if he moves 1 km towards west, he reaches D; then if he moves 2 km towards south, which place will he reach? Draw the figure and find the total distance he has to travel to reach the destination.
- c) Sangeetha built a new house with a flower garden of size 6 m × 6 m. If poles are erected at intervals of 1 m all around the garden, how many poles are needed? If each pole is 1.5 m high, what is the total height of all poles needed?
- d) To stitch one shirt for a student, 1 m 25 cm of cloth is required. How much cloth is needed to stitch shirts for 22 students?
- e) The distance between village A and village B is 3 km 450 m. The distance between village B and village C is 5 km 350 m. If a road is laid from village A to village C, what is the length of the road?
