### Exercise 6.3 (Pages 99, 100)
> **Source References:**  
> - [Page 99 HTML](file:///e:/19.03.26/new%20project/tn_school_books/5th/maths_em/term1/06_information_processing/page_99/page_99.html)  
> - [Page 100 HTML](file:///e:/19.03.26/new%20project/tn_school_books/5th/maths_em/term1/06_information_processing/page_100/page_100.html)

1. The bar chart represents the number of students using different modes of transport to school (Cycle, Walk, Car, Bus):
   - a) Which mode of transport is mostly used by the students?
   - b) How many students come to school by walk?
   - c) Which mode of transport is used the least?
   - d) How many students come to school by bus?

2. The Mathematics and Science grades of 30 students of class 5 are given in a two-way table:
   - a) How many students secured grade 'A' in both subjects?
   - b) How many students secured grade 'D' in Science compared to Mathematics?
   - c) What is the most common grade in Science?

3. The rainfall recorded in a city during a week is represented in a pictograph:
   - a) Which day recorded the maximum rainfall?
   - b) Which day recorded the minimum rainfall?
   - c) What is the rainfall recorded on Sunday?
   - d) What is the rainfall recorded on Monday?
   - e) What is the total rainfall recorded in the week?

4. The number of fish in the aquariums of Neela, Mala, Kala, and Bala are 16, 20, 12, and 24 respectively. Draw a pictograph and answer:
   - a) Who has the maximum number of fish?
   - b) Whose aquarium has 16 fish?
   - c) How many fish less does Kala have than Mala?
   - d) What is the total number of fish with Neela and Bala?
