### Section 4: Grammar — Past Perfect & Future Perfect Tenses

> **Source Reference / மூலச் சான்று:** [HTML Page](file:///e:/19.03.26/new%20project/tn_school_books/5th/english/term3/03_my_duties/page_62/page_62.html), [HTML Page](file:///e:/19.03.26/new%20project/tn_school_books/5th/english/term3/03_my_duties/page_63/page_63.html) & [HTML Page](file:///e:/19.03.26/new%20project/tn_school_books/5th/english/term3/03_my_duties/page_64/page_64.html)

#### Past Perfect Tense
##### A. Fill in the blanks.
1. I had given him the book.
2. We __________ to the park. (go)
3. You __________ to your uncle's house. (be)
4. He __________ before 5 o'clock. (sleep)
5. She ____________ the bill. (pay)
6. They ____________ him before the party. (meet)
7. It ____________ before I touched it. (break)

##### B. Complete the following sentences using past perfect tense.
1. The bus _______________ the bus stop before I went. (leave)
2. The exam _______________ when I reached the exam hall. (start)
3. She _______________ the apple as I told her to stop. (eat)
4. The satellite _______________ on the moon before they gave the command. (land)
5. Raju _______________ to the ground before others arrived. (arrive)

#### Future Perfect Tense
##### Fill in the blanks with future perfect tense.
1. I will have reached the place by 6 pm.
2. We ______________ the painting by tomorrow. (complete)
3. You ______________ the match by this time tomorrow. (win)
4. They ______________ everyone to the marriage by next week. (invite)
5. He ______________ all the money within an hour. (spend)
6. She ______________ a job in America by next year. (get)
7. It ______________ all the candies. (eat)
